Why wasn't MathML supported earlier?

Looks like the major browsers decided to not implement them until rather recently. For example, the first MathML support on the big few browsers happened on Chrome 24 back in 2012, but removed it and re-introduced it officially in Versions 109 onwards (2023). As a result, developers weren't able to make use of the <math> tag until then.
