The Very Beginning

Way, way back when I was a child, the only music that I'd really have heard would probably just be the music that my parents listened to. Vaguely, I recall long roadtrips in the car, catchy tunes of cantopop in the background. My cantonese was never really that great to begin with, so I had only a vague connection with these songs and didn't get too much into it.

Early Adolescence; the introduction of the guitar.

At the age of around 10 years old, I was introduced to the guitar. My parents had signed me up for guitar lessons that I did not want to take. It was an after-school class, with a couple other students that were older than me (this would've been around 2nd grade, with other students nearing the end of their elementary school tenure). Songs we'd learn were mostly pop songs; some that I distinctly remember would be Justin Bieber's "Baby", Teio Cruz's "Dynamite", and Katy Perry's "Firework". Though I wasn't very good at this, I grew to enjoy strumming along to these catchy tunes, which my parents had also began to play around the house.
